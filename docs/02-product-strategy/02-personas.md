## Persona 1 – Amira Hassan (Candidate)
**Age:** 27 · **Role:** Junior software developer · **Location:** Manchester, UK
**Tech comfort:** High · **Device:** Mostly mobile

**Goals**
- Find a mid-level developer role within 3 months.
- Know where each application stands without chasing recruiters.

**Frustrations**
- Applies to 30+ jobs and hears back from 3.
- Re-types the same work history on every job site.
- Has applied to a job that turned out to be a scam.

**How the Candidate Agent helps**
- Suggests jobs that match the skills in her profile.
- Drafts a tailored cover letter she edits before sending.

**Key scenario:** Amira finds a Software Engineer role, applies in under
2 minutes using her saved CV, and gets notified when she is shortlisted.

---

## Persona 2 – David Okafor (Employer / Recruiter)
**Age:** 41 · **Role:** HR Manager at ABC Ltd (80 employees)
**Tech comfort:** Medium · **Device:** Desktop at work

**Goals**
- Fill a Software Engineer vacancy within 4 weeks.
- Shortlist quickly from 100+ applications.

**Frustrations**
- Applications arrive by email and get lost.
- No easy way to compare candidates side by side.
- Hiring managers ask "where are we?" and he has no dashboard.

**How the Employer Agent helps**
- Drafts a clear job description from bullet points.
- Summarises each application. **David makes every shortlist decision.**

**Key scenario:** David posts a job, receives 100 applications, filters by
experience, shortlists 10 and invites 3 to interview, all in one dashboard.

---

## Persona 3 – Sophie Clarke (Platform Administrator)
**Age:** 34 · **Role:** Trust & Safety Lead at JobConnect
**Tech comfort:** High · **Device:** Desktop

**Goals**
- Keep fake companies and scam jobs off the platform.
- Report weekly platform health to management.

**Frustrations**
- Manually reviewing every new company is slow.
- Hard to spot patterns of fraud across many accounts.

**How the Admin Agent helps**
- Flags suspicious job posts (e.g. "pay a fee to apply") for review.
- Generates weekly activity summaries. **Sophie decides on every suspension.**

**Key scenario:** Sophie reviews 5 flagged job posts, rejects 2 scams,
suspends one fraudulent employer account, and the action is audit-logged.

## Persona-to-Feature Traceability (initial)
| Persona frustration | Feature that solves it |
|---|---|
| Amira never hears back | Application status tracking + notifications |
| Amira re-types her history | Reusable profile + saved CVs |
| Amira met a scam job | Company verification + job approval |
| David loses emails | Employer application dashboard |
| David can't compare | Filter + shortlist candidates |
| Sophie reviews manually | Admin verification queue + AI fraud flags |
