## 1. Problem Statement
Job seekers apply to vacancies and rarely hear back. Employers receive
large volumes of applications with no structured way to manage them.
Fraudulent job adverts erode trust in online job boards.

## 2. Vision Statement
**For** job seekers and hiring companies
**who** struggle with fragmented, opaque recruitment processes,
**JobConnect** is a smart job marketplace
**that** connects candidates with verified employers and gives both sides
full visibility of every application from submission to decision,
supported by role-specific AI assistants, with humans always making
the final hiring decisions.
**Unlike** generic job boards where applications disappear into a void,
**our product** offers real-time application tracking for candidates and
a structured recruitment pipeline for employers.

## 3. User Roles
- **Candidate** – searches and applies for jobs, tracks applications.
- **Employer** – manages company profile, posts jobs, manages applicants.
- **Administrator** – verifies companies, moderates jobs, manages the platform.

## 4. AI Agents (one per role)
| Agent | Purpose (initial hypothesis) | Risk level |
|---|---|---|
| Candidate Agent | Job suggestions, CV tips, cover letter drafts | Low |
| Employer Agent | Job description drafting, application summaries | High – human must decide |
| Admin Agent | Fraud flagging, activity summaries, reports | Medium |

## 5. Stakeholder Register
| Stakeholder | Type | Interest | Influence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Job Seekers | Primary user | Find jobs, apply easily, see status | High |
| Employers / Recruiters | Primary user | Fill vacancies with quality candidates | High |
| Platform Admin | Primary user | Safe, fraud-free platform | Medium |
| Product Owner | Internal | Deliver working product | Very high |
| Developer | Internal | Maintainable code, clear requirements | High |
| Data protection regulator (ICO / EU DPAs) | External | GDPR compliance | Medium |
| AI model provider | External | API terms, data handling | Medium |
| Candidates affected by AI | Special interest | Fairness, right to explanation | High |
| Email / hosting providers | External | Terms of service, deliverability | Low |

## 6. Working Assumptions
- A1: AI agents are in-product features; AI never makes final hiring decisions.
- A2: Portfolio project built to production standard, UK/EU compliant.
- A3: AI agent features are "Could have" – built after the core marketplace works (PO decision, 2026-11-11).
